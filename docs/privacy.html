Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 1, 2026

Overview

lockedIn respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Major and year (optional)
  • Dorm (optional)

Usage Data

  • Focus session duration and timestamps
  • Classes added to profile
  • Study Stack content you create
  • Interactions (follows, votes, favorites)

Device Information

  • Device type and OS version
  • App version
  • General location (country/region)

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Transcript images are deleted - If you verify grades, transcript images are deleted immediately after review.
  • We do not sell your data - Ever.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve the app
  • Display leaderboards and profiles
  • Enable social features (following, Study Stacks)
  • Send important account notifications
  • Aggregate analytics (non-personal)

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share:

  • With your consent - When you explicitly agree
  • Aggregated data - Non-identifying statistics
  • Legal requirements - If required by law

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) - Authentication, database, and file storage
  • Google Sign-In - Optional sign-in method
  • Firebase Analytics - App usage analytics

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure cloud infrastructure (Firebase/Google Cloud)
  • Access controls and authentication

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data through your profile
  • Update your information anytime
  • Delete your account and data
  • Contact us with privacy questions

Children's Privacy

lockedIn is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated through the app.

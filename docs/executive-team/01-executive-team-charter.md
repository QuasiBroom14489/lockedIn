# lockedIn Executive Team Charter

This defines a virtual executive team so you can run lockedIn like an early-stage company, even as a solo founder.

## Leadership Team

## CEO / Founder (You)
- Owns vision, priorities, fundraising, and final decisions.
- Weekly outputs:
  - Top 3 company priorities.
  - Go/no-go decisions on major bets.
  - Stakeholder updates (advisors, pilot partners, supporters).

## Chief Product Officer (CPO)
- Owns product strategy, roadmap, user research, and feature prioritization.
- Focus areas:
  - Core loop quality: start session -> complete verified focus -> social proof.
  - Student retention and motivation systems.
  - Feature sequencing tied to measurable outcomes.

## Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
- Owns architecture, velocity, reliability, and security.
- Focus areas:
  - iOS app quality (SwiftUI + MVVM).
  - Firebase data integrity and access rules.
  - Observability, crash monitoring, and release readiness.

## Chief Growth Officer (CGO)
- Owns acquisition, activation, and referral loops.
- Focus areas:
  - Notre Dame campus ambassador and club channel strategy.
  - Referral mechanics and waitlist conversion.
  - Experiment design for onboarding and virality.

## Chief Community Officer (CCO)
- Owns social trust, moderation standards, and student community programs.
- Focus areas:
  - Community guidelines and enforcement.
  - Healthy competition design.
  - Partnered events (study halls, finals sprints, accountability cohorts).

## Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- Owns runway, unit economics, monetization modeling, and funding narrative.
- Focus areas:
  - Budget and spend controls.
  - Pricing experiments for premium tiers.
  - Investor metrics package and forecast updates.

## Chief of Staff (COS)
- Owns operating cadence, cross-functional coordination, and decision hygiene.
- Focus areas:
  - Weekly business review operations.
  - Action tracking and blocker removal.
  - Decision logs and postmortems.

## Team Cadence (Operating Rhythm)

## Monday: Executive Planning (60 min)
- Review KPI dashboard.
- Confirm weekly goals by function.
- Resolve dependency blockers.

## Wednesday: Product + Engineering Review (45 min)
- Demo in-progress features.
- Review bugs and technical risks.
- Confirm release scope.

## Friday: Weekly Business Review (45 min)
- Score week vs goals.
- Review experiment outcomes.
- Capture decisions, lessons, and next-week bets.

## Decision Rules
- Metric-first: every major feature must map to at least one KPI.
- Small-batch execution: ship in 1-2 week increments.
- One owner per objective: clear accountability at all times.
- Fast loops: test on campus users before broad rollout.

## 90-Day Company Objectives (Initial)
- Launch a stable campus MVP with reliable focus tracking and social profile utility.
- Achieve early retention signals (students returning weekly).
- Build credible story and metrics for advisors, partners, or early investors.

# lockedIn Execution Toolkit

Use these artifacts weekly to keep planning and execution tight.

## KPI Scorecard (Weekly)
- Acquisition:
  - New signups
  - Invite conversion rate
- Activation:
  - Signup -> first session completion rate
- Retention:
  - D1, D7, D30 retention
  - WAU / MAU
- Engagement:
  - Sessions per WAU
  - Verified focus minutes per WAU
- Quality:
  - Crash-free sessions
  - Median screen load time

## Weekly Business Review Agenda (45 min)
1. Metrics in 5 minutes: what moved up/down.
2. Product in 10 minutes: shipped, blocked, at-risk.
3. Growth in 10 minutes: experiment results and next tests.
4. Tech health in 10 minutes: bugs, reliability, security.
5. Decisions in 10 minutes: what starts, stops, or changes.

## Launch Readiness Checklist
- Product
  - Core loop is stable end-to-end.
  - Onboarding friction points addressed.
- Engineering
  - Analytics events firing correctly.
  - Firebase rules reviewed for least privilege.
  - Crash monitoring and alert routing set.
- Growth
  - Beta cohort recruitment list ready.
  - Referral mechanic enabled and tracked.
- Community
  - Reporting and moderation workflow documented.
  - Community standards published.
- Business
  - Weekly KPI dashboard live.
  - Founder update template prepared.

## Risk Register (Top Early Risks)
- Trust risk: false or low-integrity focus sessions.
  - Mitigation: anti-abuse checks, random verification steps, anomaly detection.
- Retention risk: novelty wears off after first week.
  - Mitigation: habit loops, streak logic, social accountability prompts.
- Distribution risk: weak campus adoption.
  - Mitigation: ambassador model, club partnerships, event-based campaigns.
- Execution risk: too many features, low focus.
  - Mitigation: strict weekly priority cap and kill criteria for low-impact work.

## Hiring/Support Sequence (When Needed)
1. Part-time campus growth lead (ambassadors + events).
2. Contract iOS engineer (velocity bursts on roadmap critical path).
3. Community/moderation support as active user base scales.

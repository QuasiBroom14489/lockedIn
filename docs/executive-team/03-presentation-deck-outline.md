# lockedIn Deck Template (Simple Version)

Use this to make a clear story in plain language.  
Format for each slide:
- What to show
- What to say (30-45 seconds)

## Slide 1: Title
- What to show:
  - `lockedIn`
  - Tagline: `Students helping students study smarter`
  - Your name, role, date
- What to say:
  - "I’m building lockedIn so students can use each other’s real experience to become more effective learners."

## Slide 2: The Problem
- What to show:
  - 3 bullets:
    - Students are distracted.
    - Study habits are inconsistent.
    - Students rarely get practical, peer-tested study systems in one place.
- What to say:
  - "Students need more than a timer. They need proven tactics from peers plus accountability."

## Slide 3: Why This Matters Now
- What to show:
  - One sentence: `Students already use social apps daily. We can turn that behavior into study accountability.`
- What to say:
  - "The behavior already exists. lockedIn channels it toward productive outcomes."

## Slide 4: Product in One Minute
- What to show:
  - Flow diagram:
    - Sign up -> Start focus session -> Complete verified session -> Share what worked -> Others reuse it
- What to say:
  - "The core loop is focus plus shared learning. Each student’s progress helps everyone else improve."

## Slide 5: Current Product Status
- What to show:
  - Built:
    - iOS app from scratch
    - Auth
    - Focus sessions
    - Leaderboard
    - Social/profile features
  - Next:
    - Feed improvements
    - Better anti-cheat
    - Growth instrumentation
- What to say:
  - "Core product exists. We are now optimizing retention and growth."

## Slide 6: Target User and First Market
- What to show:
  - `First users: Notre Dame students`
  - `Why: dense social network + fast feedback loop`
- What to say:
  - "We will win one campus first, then replicate."

## Slide 7: Traction (Fill with Real Numbers)
- What to show:
  - `WAU`
  - `D7 retention`
  - `Sessions per active user`
  - `Invite rate`
  - `Crash-free rate`
- What to say:
  - "These are our operating numbers. We review them every week."

## Slide 8: Business Model (Simple)
- What to show:
  - Free core app
  - Paid upgrades to test:
    - Advanced analytics
    - Premium profile/cosmetics
    - Group challenge tools
- What to say:
  - "We start free for growth, then test paid value once retention is stable."

## Slide 9: Go-To-Market Plan
- What to show:
  - Step 1: ambassadors and study clubs
  - Step 2: referral loop in product
  - Step 3: repeatable campus launch playbook
- What to say:
  - "Distribution is campus-by-campus with student-led growth."

## Slide 10: Why We Can Win
- What to show:
  - 3 advantages:
    - Verified focus
    - Peer learning loops
    - Campus density strategy
- What to say:
  - "Most tools are solo. lockedIn combines verified effort with shared student playbooks."

## Slide 11: Next 6 Months
- What to show:
  - Months 1-2: product stability + beta
  - Months 3-4: retention improvements
  - Months 5-6: monetization tests + expansion prep
- What to say:
  - "Execution is phased. We focus on one major objective at a time."

## Slide 12: Clear Ask
- What to show:
  - Pick one ask per audience:
    - Advisors: intros and strategic guidance
    - Partners: pilot with student groups
    - Investors: funding + GTM support
- What to say:
  - "Here is exactly how you can help us in the next 30 days."

## Presentation Tips (Non-Business Founder Friendly)
- Keep each slide to one main point.
- Use real product screenshots over abstract text.
- Say numbers plainly: "25% of users came back in 7 days."
- End with one clear ask, not multiple vague asks.

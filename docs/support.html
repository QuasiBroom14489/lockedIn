Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a focus session?

Open the app and go to the Focus tab. Set your desired duration and tap "Start Session". The timer will begin and you'll earn points for completed sessions.

How do status tiers work?

You earn points by studying and sharing. As you accumulate points, you progress through Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, and Obsidian tiers. Higher tiers unlock profile customization options.

What is a Study Stack?

Your Study Stack is a collection of tools, apps, and techniques that help you study effectively. Share yours so other students can discover resources that work.

How do I link my Spotify playlist?

Go to your Profile and tap "Edit Profile". You can paste a Spotify playlist URL that others can tap to discover your study music.

How do I delete my account?

Contact us at zj14489@gmail.com with your account email and we'll process your deletion request. All your data will be permanently removed.

The app isn't working correctly. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, make sure you have the latest version installed. Still having trouble? Email us with details about what's happening.
